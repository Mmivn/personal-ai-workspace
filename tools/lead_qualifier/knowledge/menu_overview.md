# Menu overview

Prices on the official menu are shown in Vietnamese đồng (VND). The official
site provides menu pages in Russian and English.

## Available sections

- Breakfast
- Starters
- Salads
- Soups
- Hot starters
- Main courses
- Grill
- Side dishes and sauces
- Coffee and juices
- Lemonades and tea
- Cocktails
- Wine
- Beer and spirits
- Children's menu
- Desserts

Breakfast is served until 13:00. The restaurant describes its menu as including
grilled meat, seafood, homemade desserts, and cocktails.

Individual dish names, ingredients, and prices must be taken from the current
official menu pages and verified before being used in customer-facing answers.
